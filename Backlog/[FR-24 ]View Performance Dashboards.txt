As a Scrum Master, I want to view performance dashboards so that I can monitor team activity easily and identify any issues quickly.

Dashboard is the landing page after Scrum Master logs in

Dashboard updates dynamically without page refresh

Shows real-time key metrics:

Sprint progress (burndown chart, completion percentage)

Team workload distribution (tasks per developer)

Task completion status (To Do / In Progress / In Review / Done)

Velocity trend over last 3 sprints

Risk indicators (overdue tasks, workload imbalance)

All charts and widgets are interactive (hover for details, click to drill down)
